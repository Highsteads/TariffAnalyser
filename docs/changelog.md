# Version history

The newest version is at the top.

## 1.9.5 — 23 September 2026

The plugin no longer fails to start when Indigo's server is slow to answer. It used to ask the server where its folders are the moment it loaded, and when six plugins were restarted together the server did not answer in time, so Tariff Analyser did not start at all. It now asks once it is running, keeps trying until it gets an answer, and logs one warning in the meantime. Nothing changes when the server answers straight away.

## 1.9.4 — 11 September 2026

The note inside the plugin of where its code lives on GitHub uses the same spelling as other Indigo plugins. Nothing else changed.

## 1.9.3 — 8 August 2026

The **About** item in the Plugins menu opens this project's page. It went nowhere before.

## 1.9.2 — 21 July 2026

Log lines no longer come out with the time printed twice, and a setting saved as the word "false" is read as off.

## 1.9.1 — 21 July 2026

Warnings and errors appear in the Event Log as warnings and errors. Before, they all appeared as ordinary lines.

## 1.9 — 18 July 2026

- New **Test Octopus API Connection** menu item, which checks the plugin can find the Agile prices for your region before you run a comparison.
- The comparison report gives the date the typical tariff prices were last checked, and says that Tracker and Agile use real prices.

## 1.8 — 18 July 2026

More automatic tests, covering the time bands, the gas sums and the change to and from British Summer Time. Nothing changed in what you see.

## 1.7 — 18 July 2026

- **Tariffs are compared fairly.** Every tariff is priced over the same half-hours, with the standing charge for those half-hours only. Before, a tariff with gaps in its prices could come out cheapest because some of your use was never counted.
- A tariff with prices for less than half the period is shown as **insufficient price data to rank** instead of being ranked at £0.
- In the nightly collection, a day with no Tracker price is left blank instead of being recorded as costing nothing, the Go and Flux savings include their standing charges, and the first half-hours of each day are no longer lost in British Summer Time.
- The Energy Summary uses the export tariff chosen in the settings instead of always 12p.
- The first automatic tests.

## 1.6 — 10 June 2026

Tidying of the code, and an automatic check on every change. Nothing changed in what you see.

## 1.5 — 5 June 2026

**Open report in LibreOffice when generated by a schedule/action** works. Before, the report failed to open every time that setting was ticked.

## 1.4 — 23 May 2026

Every log line starts with the time to the thousandth of a second, with a menu item to turn that off.

## 1.3 — 23 May 2026

The Octopus details can be typed into the plugin's settings, for anyone who does not keep them in `IndigoSecrets.py`. When both have a value, the file wins.

## 1.2 — 10 May 2026

- The Plugins menu is down to the Energy Summary, the Tariff Comparison and Show Plugin Info. The separate daily, weekly, monthly, yearly and custom reports became the Energy Summary.
- The Tariff Comparison asks for a single period, such as the last 30 days, instead of two dates.
- The Energy Summary shows yesterday as well, and this week runs from Monday.
- Reports are web pages that open in your browser. The spreadsheet files are gone.

## 1.1 — 6 May 2026

The nightly collection of Octopus meter readings at 2am, with one line per day in the energy database, and an energy dashboard page.

## 1.0 — 2 May 2026

First version: a comparison of UK tariffs against the energy SigenEnergyManager recorded, saved as a spreadsheet file to open in LibreOffice or Numbers.
